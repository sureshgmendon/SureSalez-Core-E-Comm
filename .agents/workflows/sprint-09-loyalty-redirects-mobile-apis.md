# Sprint 09: Loyalty Points & VIP Tiers, 301 Redirects & Headless Mobile App APIs

## Objective
Accelerate customer retention through a gamified loyalty points program, protect search equity during catalog migrations with high-performance 301 redirect mapping, and provide headless mobile APIs for iOS and Android native apps.

## Deliverables
1. **Gamified Loyalty & VIP Tiers**:
   - Points earning rules (spend \$1 = 10 pts, social share, product review with photo, birthday bonus).
   - VIP tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum) with tiered perks (free express shipping, early drop access).
   - Dynamic points-to-currency redemption slider in cart and checkout.
2. **High-Performance 301 Redirect Engine**:
   - Regex-based and exact-match URL redirect rules stored in an optimized index table.
   - 404 error hit logging and automated AI-assisted redirect suggestion.
   - Sub-millisecond redirect evaluation before WordPress query parser execution.
3. **Headless Mobile App REST & GraphQL APIs**:
   - Ultra-fast tokenized authentication (JWT & Biometric OAuth).
   - Comprehensive endpoints for product feed, push notification tokens, wishlists, and native checkout webviews.
