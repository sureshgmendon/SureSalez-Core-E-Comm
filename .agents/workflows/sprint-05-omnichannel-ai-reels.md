# Sprint 05: Omnichannel Marketplace Feeds, AI Chat Commerce & Shoppable Reels

## Objective
Expand commercial reach across social channels and marketplaces through automated product catalog syndication feeds, an AI sales chat assistant, and interactive shoppable video reels.

## Deliverables
1. **Automated Product Feed Syndication**:
   - Google Merchant Center XML feed generator with scheduled differential updates.
   - Meta Catalog (Facebook/Instagram Shops) CSV/XML feed with custom category mappings.
   - TikTok Shop catalog sync API.
2. **AI Conversational Sales Assistant**:
   - Lightweight chat widget powered by LLM embeddings of the product catalog and FAQ database.
   - Semantic product search and personalized product recommendations in chat.
   - Add-to-cart direct action inside conversational thread.
3. **Shoppable Video Reels**:
   - Mobile-first vertical video player with product hot-spot tags.
   - Swipeable reel carousel for homepage and landing page block embeds.
   - 1-tap quick buy modal for tagged reel products.
