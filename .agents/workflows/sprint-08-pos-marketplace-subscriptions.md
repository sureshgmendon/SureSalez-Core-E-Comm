# Sprint 08: In-Store POS Touch Interface, Multi-Vendor Marketplace & Subscriptions

## Objective
Provide omnichannel in-store point of sale, multi-vendor marketplace commission and vendor management, and recurring product subscription lifecycles.

## Deliverables
1. **In-Store Touch POS Interface**:
   - Progressive Web App (PWA) touch-first interface for retail staff.
   - Barcode/QR scanner integration (camera and hardware scanner support).
   - Offline-capable IndexedDB cart with automatic background sync when reconnected.
   - Receipt thermal printer support and split payment tenders (cash + card).
2. **Multi-Vendor Marketplace Engine**:
   - Vendor onboarding, isolated vendor dashboard, and catalog submission moderation.
   - Automated commission split calculation and vendor payout ledger.
3. **Product Subscription Engine**:
   - Flexible billing intervals (Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annual).
   - Customer subscription self-management (skip delivery, swap flavor/variant, pause, cancel).
   - Failed payment retry dunning scheduler.
