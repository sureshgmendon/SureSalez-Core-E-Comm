# Sprint 07: B2B Wholesale Commerce, Organization Accounts & RFQ Quote Engine

## Objective
Support enterprise B2B purchasing workflows, corporate account hierarchies, tier-based custom price lists, and Request-For-Quote (RFQ) negotiation.

## Deliverables
1. **Company & Organization Accounts**:
   - Multi-user corporate accounts with role permissions (Admin, Buyer, Approver).
   - Order spending limits and internal managerial approval workflows.
2. **Tiered B2B Price Lists & Bulk Discounts**:
   - Custom customer-group pricing matrices (e.g., Tier 1: -15%, Tier 2: -30%).
   - Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) and volume step breaks (e.g., 50+, 500+, 1000+ units).
   - Tax-exempt certificate validation and Net-30/Net-60 payment terms.
3. **RFQ (Request For Quote) Negotiation Engine**:
   - "Convert Cart to Quote" customer flow.
   - Merchant quote adjustment dashboard (line-item discounting, custom freight terms, expiry date).
   - 1-click quote acceptance and order conversion.
